Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Short version. You sign in with Steam; we get your public SteamID, name and avatar, never your password. Demos you upload are stored on our server and analysed there, visible only to your team. Raw demo files are deleted within days; the analysed data is kept while your plan allows (7 days on the free plan, while you subscribe on paid plans).

If you use AI reviews or match chat (paid plans), the analysis of that match is sent to Anthropic to write the text. The desktop app keeps everything on your computer and only checks your subscription with us. We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Who we are

RetakeHQ is an independent software project run from West Virginia, USA. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of RetakeHQ. Contact: hello@retakehq.com.

RetakeHQ comes in two forms: the web app (you use it in your browser; your team's data is stored on our server) and the desktop app (included with paid plans; your data is stored on your own computer). This policy covers both, plus this website.

1. Your account (Steam sign-in)

You sign in on Steam's own website. Steam tells us your SteamID (a public number identifying your Steam account). We then look up your public Steam profile name and avatar to show them in the app. We never see or store your Steam password.

We keep: your SteamID, name and avatar, when you joined and last signed in, your team and role, and your active sign-ins (each with the date and a device description such as "Firefox on Windows" so you can sign out other devices; we don't log your IP address against your account).

2. Demos you upload (web app)

A demo is a recording of a Counter-Strike match. When your team uploads one, we store the file and analyse it: rounds, positions, kills, damage, grenades, economy and so on. Demos contain the in-game names and SteamIDs of everyone in the match, including opponents; that information is part of the analysed data.

  • Who sees it: your uploads, their analysis, and your team's notes, comments and drawings are visible only to members of your team (and to us, to run and support the service). Professional matches we add ourselves are visible to everyone. Features that compare matches (scouting, stacked rounds, round search and "rounds like this one") only draw on those professional matches and your own team's uploads, never on another team's.
  • How long we keep it:
    WhatHow long
    The raw demo fileDeleted 3 days after it's analysed (7 days if analysis failed, so it can be retried)
    Analysed data, free planDeleted 7 days after upload
    Analysed data, paid plansKept while your team subscribes
    After a paid subscription endsKept, read-only, for 90 days; after that the free plan's rule applies
    Notes, comments, drawingsDeleted with the match they belong to, or when you delete them
  • Used, anonymised, to improve RetakeHQ (unless your team turns on private mode): we keep a copy of the match data parsed from your uploads (positions, kills, damage, grenades, economy, round results) with every player name, SteamID and team name replaced by a random code, and without the demo file, your notes, comments, drawings or AI chats. We use these copies only inside RetakeHQ, to train and check our models -- for example to make win chances more accurate for matches like yours, and to compare a player with others at a similar level. They are never shown to another team, published, sold, or used to rebuild a particular match for anyone.
  • Comparisons with pros stay pro-only: every "pros on this map do X" number is learned only from the professional matches we add ourselves; uploads never become part of it.
  • How long the training copy is kept: up to 24 months from upload, even after your team's own copy of the match is deleted. Because it carries no names or IDs, we can't link it back to you or your team. A model already trained keeps what it learned in general, but not your matches.
  • We never publish or sell your demos, your team's data, or anything that identifies you or your team.

3. AI reviews and chat

On paid plans, RetakeHQ can write a coach-style review of a player's match, answer questions about a match, about an opponent's scouting report or about Counter-Strike in general ("Ask the coach"), and read a round search our own rules can't. To do that we send the analysis involved (for a match: player names, rounds, kills, positions, stats and the patterns we found; for an opponent: the scouting report you're looking at; for a general question: your question and what the professional matches show; for a search: its words and the team names in those matches; never a raw demo file) to Anthropic, whose Claude models write the text. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this data; see Anthropic's privacy policy. Reviews we write are saved with the match so they're only generated once. On the showcase pro matches, reviews are written by us in advance and shown to everyone.

Questions and answers are saved with your team: the question, the answer, who on the team asked it, and a note if you mark an answer as wrong. We read answers marked wrong, and a sample of others, to find and fix mistakes in how RetakeHQ answers; never to train a model. They're deleted with the match they're about, when your team is deleted, and your name comes off them if you leave the team or delete your account.

If you'd rather not have a match sent for AI review, simply don't use those buttons or the chat; nothing is sent automatically except that a paid team member's own review of a demo they upload is written when it's analysed.

4. The desktop app

The desktop app analyses demos on your own computer. Your demos, analysis, notes and settings are stored there; we never receive them. The app connects to us only to check that your subscription is active: it signs in to your RetakeHQ account and sends a token for this computer and its name (so you can see which computers use your account). It keeps working offline for up to 7 days between checks. If you set up FACEIT import, the app talks directly to FACEIT for your match history and demos, under FACEIT's privacy policy.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are sold through Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record: it takes your payment, handles sales tax and sends receipts. It shares with us your name, email address, country and subscription details so we can apply your plan and help you. We never see your card or bank details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

6. Cookies

The web app uses only cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived one that remembers where to send you after signing in (for example, a team invite link). No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. This website (retakehq.com's information pages) uses no cookies.

7. Where data is stored and who helps us

  • Contabo GmbH runs the server that stores the web app's data, in Germany.
  • Anthropic writes AI reviews and chat answers (section 3).
  • Lemon Squeezy handles payments (section 5).
  • Steam (Valve) handles sign-in and provides public profile names and avatars.
  • Leetify: when you open a player's card, we look up that player's public Leetify profile by their SteamID (a public number) to show their Leetify ratings. Leetify only returns profiles its players have left public (leetify.com).
  • GitHub Pages and Google Fonts serve this website; like any host they receive your IP address and browser details when you load a page.

We use them only to provide RetakeHQ. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it except with these providers or when the law requires it. If you're outside the US, your data may be processed in the US or the other countries these providers operate in.

8. Security

Traffic to the web app is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored only as secure hashes, demo files are stored under random-looking names, and each team's data is kept separate. We back the database up regularly. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your notes, comments and uploads' analysis from your team at any time.
  • Delete your account yourself on your account page. That removes your profile, sign-ins, linked computers and AI reviews of your play; if you're the only member of your team, it also removes the team's uploads and notes, otherwise the team keeps them. (A team with an active subscription must cancel it first.)
  • Ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to correct it, at hello@retakehq.com.
  • Private mode: a team owner can turn it on in the account page. From then on, the team's uploads are never copied for training, and the training copies already made from the team's uploads are deleted before our next training run.
  • If you appear in a demo someone else uploaded and want your data removed from our analysis, email us and we'll take care of it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights; we'll honour requests made under those laws.

10. Children

RetakeHQ isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top; for significant changes we'll also let signed-in users and subscribers know before they take effect.
