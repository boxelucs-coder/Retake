Retake
Private testing · launching for teams soon

Know what they'll run before the round starts.

Retake turns your team's match demos into round-by-round tactical review and opponent scouting reports. See each team's tendencies, how their rounds open, and where you lose rounds. Built for amateur and semi-pro teams that don't have an analyst.

Get in touch

What it does

2D round viewer

Replay any round from above with smooth playback, live utility, and a whole-round utility plan colour-coded by side.

Opponent scouting

Groups each opponent's rounds by how they open: defaults, rushes, splits, stacks. You see what they tend to run on each map and economy.

Win probability with reasons

A round-by-round win probability that explains itself: which situation cost the round and what changed the odds.

Team review

Flag rounds, leave notes, and review your own play next to the same breakdown you'd get for an opponent.

How it works

  1. Bring your matches. Upload demos, or link your account so your matches come in automatically. We only fetch matches you played in or are scouting.
  2. We parse them. Every round is broken down into positions, utility, economy, and the outcome.
  3. Review and prepare. Watch rounds, read the scouting report, and go into your next match knowing what's coming.

Data & privacy

Raw demo files are deleted after 30 days; we keep only the derived round statistics your team needs for review. Your team's data is visible only to your team. We never sell individual player data.
